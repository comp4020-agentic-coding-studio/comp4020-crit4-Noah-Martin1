# Reflections

### 1. What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

I was initially following a theme based around orbs that would orbit each other, grow, and trigger ping sounds that the user could interact with. While I still think this could have become a decent website, I realised that it would be very difficult to execute well. The site Claude had created was technically close to my vision, but something about it did not feel right. Instead of continuing to try to fix a concept that I was no longer confident in, I decided to change direction entirely. I moved towards a more straightforward, instrument-based experience with simpler and more direct user interactions. This gave me a much clearer direction for the project and made the interaction feel more intentional.

### 2. What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

This work reinforced that it is okay to change an idea during development. I have learned that spending more time trying to force an idea to work is not always the best approach. Sometimes an idea has reached its limit, does not fit the purpose of the project, or simply is not producing the experience I wanted. I want to become a developer who can recognise this early and be willing to replace an idea with a different solution rather than becoming attached to the original concept. I think being able to adapt and change direction is an important part of developing good software.
